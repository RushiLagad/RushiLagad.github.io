Low-biomass tumor microbiome data are easy to over-read: contaminants and batch structure can look like biology. PDAC-EcoMap grades the evidence for each intratumoral microbial signal in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma before it is allowed into an interpretable, leakage-robust model.

**Talk.** Lagad RR, Modha N, Pathak D, Goswami A. PDAC-EcoMap: contamination-aware evidence gating for leakage-robust, interpretable machine learning on the intratumoral microbiome in pancreatic ductal adenocarcinoma. Selected abstract talk, Session V (AI in Biology), Dr. Robert Gillies Machine Learning Workshop in Cancer, Moffitt Cancer Center, Tampa, FL. October 15 to 16, 2026.
