Project co-lead of an eight-person, multi-institution team at the NIAID-BRC AI Codeathon 2.0, Argonne National Laboratory (September 16 to 18, 2026). The team built an agent workflow that takes a biological hypothesis, finds relevant public omics datasets, and runs a reproducible analysis to test it.
