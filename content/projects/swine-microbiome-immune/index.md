**Poster.** Modha N, Lagad RR, Goswami A, Tsai TC, Howe S, Watson A, Patel S. Modeling gut microbiome-immune co-development using multi-omics and machine learning in swine. Arkansas Biosciences Institute Annual Research Symposium, Fayetteville, AR. October 16, 2026.
