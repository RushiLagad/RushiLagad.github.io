abstract: Understanding causal relationships between the rumen microbiome and host phenotypes is critical for improving livestock productivity. We applied two-sample Mendelian Randomization (MR) to identify causal microbial taxa influencing feed efficiency and methane production in beef cattle. Using whole-genome sequencing data and 16S rRNA amplicon profiles from over 400 animals, we identified several microbial genera with significant causal effects on host metabolic traits. Our results provide actionable targets for microbiome-based interventions in livestock systems.
