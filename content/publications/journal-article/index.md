abstract: Bacterial gene cassettes are mobile genetic elements that play a critical role in horizontal gene transfer and antimicrobial resistance. We developed Cassette2Vec, a representation learning framework that embeds gene cassettes into dense vector representations using a variational autoencoder (VAE) architecture. These embeddings capture functional and evolutionary relationships between cassettes, enabling downstream tasks including clustering, classification, and novel cassette discovery. Applied to large-scale integron and cassette databases, Cassette2Vec demonstrates superior performance over traditional sequence-based methods in predicting cassette function and tracing evolutionary lineages.
